Build What You Believe In

Software-Engineer  |  Content-Creator

I believe in people

After more than ten years in customer service, I’ve learned how much impact people can have on one another through positive interaction and perspective. A conversation, encouragement, or simply helping someone feel understood can change the direction of their day—and sometimes their life.

Fear of failure can keep talented people from taking the first step toward what they actually want, and I know that feeling because I’ve had to push through that fear myself.

There were times in my life when I needed the same perspective from outside. I wouldn’t be where I am today without the gift of illumination a few great people have bestowed upon me. Meeting the right people can truly transform your outlook, and sometimes that’s all it takes to bloom.

Your digital frontier.

Technology is one of the ways I help others present ideas to the world. I work with people to turn ambitions into digital spaces that represent them authentically.

I listen first, understand what they want to communicate, and translate that into design, functionality, and content. The goal is simple: create something that feels like a genuine extension of the person or brand behind it and in a way that works well.

Lets build your brand.

I build custom websites and applications for businesses, individuals, and people with ideas they want to put into the world. As I continue growing my portfolio, I’m looking to work with ambitious people who are ready to establish or strengthen their presence online.

If you have a business, an idea, a story, or something you’re ready to pursue, I’d love to help you bring it to life.
