About Me

I believe in people, sometimes more than they do

After more than ten years in customer service, I’ve learned how much impact people can have on one another through positive interaction and perspective. A conversation, encouragement, or simply helping someone feel understood can change the direction of their day—and sometimes their life.

Fear of failure can keep talented people from taking the first step toward what they actually want, and I know that feeling because I’ve had to push through it myself.

I’m a self-taught software engineer, bartender, and lifelong learner. I’ve built my path by learning, experimenting, failing, adapting, and continuing forward. I try to help others the same way—not by telling them who to become, but by helping illuminate a path they can choose to walk.

Your digital presence should feel like you.

Technology is one of the ways I do that. I work with people to turn their ideas, businesses, and ambitions into digital spaces that represent them authentically.

I listen first, understand what they want to communicate, and translate that into design, functionality, and content. The goal is simple: create something that feels like a genuine extension of the person or brand behind it.

Let me help you build your brand.

I build custom websites and applications for businesses, individuals, and people with ideas they want to put into the world. As I continue growing my portfolio, I’m looking to work with ambitious people who are ready to establish or strengthen their presence online.

The digital world keeps moving whether we feel ready or not. You don’t need to wait until you feel completely prepared.

If you have a business, an idea, a story, or something you’re ready to pursue, I’d love to help you bring it to life.
